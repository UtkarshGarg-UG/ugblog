The Problem: With condition number κ = 20, the gradient in the steep direction (θ₁) is 20× larger than in the flat direction (θ₂).

Watch the optimizer oscillate back and forth in the steep direction while making painfully slow progress toward the minimum. This is exactly what orthogonalization fixes!
