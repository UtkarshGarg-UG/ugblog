Quadratic Convergence: Because φ'(1) = 0, the Newton-Schulz iteration has quadratic convergence near σ = 1. Each iteration roughly squares the error: if |σₖ - 1| = 0.1, then |σₖ₊₁ - 1| ≈ 0.01.

This means just 5 iterations are enough to get very close to 1 from almost any starting point in (0, √3).
