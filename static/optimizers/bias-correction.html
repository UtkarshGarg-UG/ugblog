Why this matters: At step 1, we're computing m = β×0 + (1-β)×g = 0.1×g. We're averaging the gradient with zeros from "before training started"! The correction factor 1/(1-β^t) compensates for this. As t→∞, β^t→0, so the correction factor→1 (no correction needed once we have enough history).
