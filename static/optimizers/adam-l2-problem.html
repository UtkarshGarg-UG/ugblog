⚠️ The Core Problem

With L2, the regularization term λw becomes part of the gradient, so Adam's adaptive scaling divides it by √v:

Effective decay = λw / (√v + ε)

w₁: 0.01 × 2 / 5.0 = 0.004
w₂: 0.01 × 2 / 0.5 = 0.040

10× difference in decay for identical weights!

💡 Key Insight

Large weights should decay consistently regardless of their gradient history. But with Adam+L2, weights in steep regions get under-regularized while weights in flat regions get over-regularized. AdamW fixes this by applying decay outside the adaptive mechanism.
