Why AdamW Wins

Adam+L2 scales weight decay by 1/√v, causing inconsistent regularization. AdamW applies weight decay after the adaptive update, ensuring all weights decay at the same rate λ·w regardless of their gradient history. This leads to better generalization, especially in large models.
